Everything in the brief — plus all seven bonuses

CORE REQUIREMENTS

  • Configurable rainfall — steady / heavy / cloudburst, 5–200 mm/h, or the real Sept 2022 storm
  • City as connected regions — 315 cells of 500 m, 4-neighbour flow, open boundaries
  • Accumulation and movement — mass-conserving storage network, water balance to 10⁻⁶ m³
  • Drainage capacity and terrain — per-cell drains; 90 m Copernicus DEM, 9 samples per cell
  • Water levels over time — engine steps ≤ 1 s, saved frames played back in 3D
  • Safe / Warning / Critical — 10 cm and 30 cm depth thresholds, adjustable
  • Time-based visualisation — 3D atlas, time slider, flood-progression chart
  • Critical regions and time to critical — warnings table with countdown and lead time

ALL 7 BONUS FEATURES

  • Normal rainfall
  • Heavy rainfall
  • Drainage failure
  • Blocked drainage channel
  • Scenario comparison
  • Affected population (exposure)
  • Interactive time slider

What we added beyond the brief

1 AI surrogate

Neural network trained on 4,000 of our own engine runs. Predicts peak depth within 5.6 cm on 572 held-out runs (R² 0.995) and scores 609 response plans instantly.

2 Response planning

Drain upgrades, upstream detention, pumps and drain clearing, compared on the same storm: peak depth −21.5 cm, 0.85 million m³ less standing water.

3 Verification

21 automated engine checks, water balance to 10⁻⁶ m³, convergence under a halved time step, and a sensitivity sweep: 29–31 cells whichever way assumptions move.

4 Reality check

Replay of the real 4–5 Sept 2022 storm against reported flood sites: 3 of 5 hit, but 51% by chance — so we report it as not yet better than chance.
